Compatibility report

What survived the port

Every material source behaviour is classified. Nothing is silently dropped.

Preserved exactly

11
  • Five killzones with independent session windows, colours, names, and box/text transparency.
  • High / low / midpoint pivots with "Until Mitigated" and "Past Mitigation" extension.
  • "Most Recent" vs "All" pivot scope, mitigation invalidation, and right-side labels.
  • Killzone range table with rolling averages and 9 table positions.
  • Day / week / month opens, previous highs and lows, full-height separators.
  • Eight opening-price sessions plus four vertical timestamp lines.
  • Six HTF candle panels (5m → 1W) with custom daily-open modes.
  • FVG and volume-imbalance boxes between HTF candles.
  • Trace lines for open, close, high, and low with per-line style and width.
  • HTF labels and close countdowns in true HH:MM:SS time.
  • Every colour, width, style, toggle, and default mapped 1:1.

Approximated

5
  • Alerts are visual only. Broken-pivot and DWM high/low conditions still evaluate; no alert side effects are emitted.
  • HTF aggregation rebuilds candles locally from chart bars, with exchange-timezone day, week and month boundaries.
  • Day/week/month boundaries use exchange-timezone midnight and Monday starts; venue session opens and holiday calendars are not reproduced.
  • Countdown clock is derived from the current HTF bar close instead of timenow.
  • Pine tables are painted on the canvas with the same rows, columns, and placement.

Removed openly

5
  • alert() and alertcondition() calls; the indicator calculates and draws only.
  • Pine drawing-object handles replaced by per-repaint canvas geometry.
  • Broker/server time, ticks, Bid/Ask, and spread are unavailable in the sandbox.
  • Chart mutation and persistence — no I/O, storage, timers, or network calls.
  • 500-object hard cap is enforced by the max-days session limit.
No blockers. Placement: Overlay: on (main chart). Precision: symbol default. Output: one Inputs JSON schema and one JavaScript factory function.
